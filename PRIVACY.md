# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 10, 2026_

## Introduction

Driver Exam ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

## Information We Collect

- Study progress and quiz scores — stored locally on your device.
- App preferences (theme, language, font size) — stored locally on your device.
- Anonymous device identifier — used solely for feedback attribution so we can follow up on issues you report.
- Feedback and bug reports — submitted voluntarily and may include a screenshot and device info (OS version, app version).

## Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personally identifiable information. We do not use advertising trackers or sell your data to third parties.

## Data Storage

All study data and preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and SecureStore. We do not upload your study progress to any server. Feedback reports are sent to our server and stored securely.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses Expo services for push notifications and over-the-air updates. These services may collect anonymous usage data as described in Expo's privacy policy. We do not integrate any advertising SDKs.

## Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users of driving age (typically 15+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@umaru.cc.
